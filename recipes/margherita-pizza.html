Margherita Pizza

Description

The classic pizza from Naples, made with just a few simple ingredients: a thin crust, tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella and basil. Its colours reflect the Italian flag.

Baking it at a high temperature gives you a crisp base with a soft, chewy edge.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
  • 3/4 cup tomato sauce
  • 200g fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and sugar in a large bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil and stir to form a dough.
  2. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour, until doubled in size.
  4. Preheat the oven to its highest setting (around 240°C / 475°F) with a baking tray inside.
  5. Divide the dough into two and stretch or roll each piece into a thin round.
  6. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a border for the crust, then top with the mozzarella.
  7. Slide the pizza onto the hot tray and bake for 8 to 12 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  8. Top with fresh basil leaves, drizzle with olive oil and serve immediately.
